Two approaches

A deterministic file, and a loop that may only teach what Lean already took.

Generator

Stages 0-8. The theorem is a tree, then Lean syntax. The same seed produces the same sentences. That file is not the model's corpus. This road has no loop database. Default CLI: concrete theorems about Nat. Also richgen, generate_general, the double definition, assess_hypothesis, and explore.

Lean as judge

The loop reads proved sentences, takes a step, and samples text. A text with no equation does not go to Lean. A new equation is checked as a theorem closed by rfl. An accepted new formula can receive a step. A rejection is stored and is not taught. No other model is called.

Weights and the database live under var/, which is not in git. Do not resume run-1, run-2, run-3, or run-4.

What a run can show

Loss, new equations, Lean's verdict. Not the learning flag.

  • Whether loss on the proved corpus falls through the writing gate, and whether it stays there after the corpus gets richer.
  • Whether sentences the model writes are new, and whether Lean accepts them.
  • Whether accepted ones get deeper, in the same syntactic sense as the generator, or stay bare equalities.
  • learning: yes is not one of those outcomes. The champion is champion-generator-v0. The held-out pair is 0 = 0 and the negation of 1 = 0, already scored 1.0. diversity and resource are the constants 1.
  • A good run of the model's own equations does not flip that flag. A bad run does not flip it either.

run-3 · the rhythm

Twenty formulas, then up to two rounds of four endings.

  • New run, new weights. Prompt <|propose|>. Temperature 1, nucleus 0.9. One argmax line stored apart, not in the verdict table.
  • One training step on up to four proved generator sentences. The model did not write until it had taken one step on sentences Lean had already accepted.
  • While more than 128 proved sentences sat ahead of the first read, the generator opened no new batch.
  • A text with no equation is not a formula. A repeat stays in the run. A new equation goes to Lean as theorem model_eq : … := rfl.
  • If none of the twenty was accepted, four endings continue from the formula Lean had read furthest into. A second four opens only when one ending gets strictly further. No third round. The rfl is stapled by the loop.

What the 90-minute check counted

Training up, difficulty up, or a freeze.

  • Training up: lm_state.step increased and last_loss is lower than the previous check.
  • Difficulty up: curriculum level rose, or the latest proved generate rows have a higher mean depth or size. Hard caps already seen: depth 3 and size 9 for generate, depth 6 for richgen.
  • Maximum: curriculum level 7 with its gate passed, or the generators are on those caps and the general surface cannot grow.
  • Level 2 asks for 1000 evaluated generate_general rows. That generator had 9 fixed statements, so level 7 was not reachable without a new generator. The 1000 gate was not lowered.
  • The approach works only if, at that maximum, the model has a Lean-accepted sentence that is not the original character put back and not a copy of a generator line.

run-4 verdict

Curriculum reached the top. The writing approach did not.

Level 7, the top of this program, was reached at 09:37 after general sentences started to vary. 9296 of them were proved at that check. The 1000 gate was not lowered. Concrete generate rows stay depth 3 and size at most 9. Loss on the fresh read was about 0.46. The model was not rereading.
